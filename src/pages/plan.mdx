# Rehab-to-performance plan

*Template v0. Gates, not calendars. Every number is a labelled opening position, replaced by your own measurements as your log fills. This page describes the plan; it stores nothing. To keep your own log, download the [tracker spreadsheet or printable log sheet](/templates/).*

**This site teaches. It does not diagnose, clear a symptom, or replace a clinician.** Every rule below keys on load, volume, range and preparedness relative to your own history, never on "this movement is bad". If you have no clinical diagnosis and no imaging, the plan still works, and it says so where it matters. Read the red flags first; they end self-direction and go to a clinician the same day, at any phase.

<Disclaimer />

## How to read the gates

Each phase ends in exit gates. A gate is met when your log shows it, not when you feel it is. The tag after each gate names where the number comes from: a source id from the site registry, `[heuristic]` for a rule of thumb without a measured source, or `[proposed]` for an opening position you are expected to replace with your own data. The [tracker spreadsheet](/templates/) has a sheet for marking gates met.

## Phase 0 — Baseline (2 weeks)

The point of Phase 0 is to turn guesses into a log. Nothing changes in training yet.

- Red-flag screen, written down, repeated monthly.
- Daily check-in, training day or not: a morning stiffness test performed identically each day, anything felt below the glute (yes/no), hours slept, life stress, bodyweight.
- McGill endurance battery: trunk flexor hold, Biering-Sørensen extensor hold, side bridge left and right. Record seconds and the ratios. <Source id="mcgill-1999-endurance-norms" />
- Film squat, deadlift and RDL at working loads; compare monthly.
- Daily walking minutes.

The four holds give three ratios: flexor/extensor, side bridge/extensor, and left/right side-bridge asymmetry. The same ratios decide the Phase 1 and Phase 2 ratio gates. The [tracker spreadsheet](/templates/) works them out from your hold times.

## Phase 1 — Capacity floor (4–8 weeks)

- Daily: walk 20–30 min `[proposed]`; McGill big 3 as endurance work (descending pyramid, e.g. 5-3-1 reps of 10 s holds), done at least an hour after waking because the disc is most hydrated and stiffest in flexion early in the day `[heuristic]`.
- Two non-consecutive axial-loading days. Main lifts at RPE ≤ 7, no grinding `[proposed]`. <Source id="helms-rpe-rir" /> Squat around 65–75% `[proposed]`; pulls from blocks or trap bar; RDLs as the hinge driver, double progression 3×8 → 3×12 `[proposed]`.
- Loaded carries (farmer's, suitcase) and anti-rotation work twice a week `[proposed]`.

**Regression rule** `[heuristic]`. A bad next-day verdict drops one rung on that movement, in this order: **range first, then moment arm, then load. Never the movement's identity.** A hinge stays a hinge; it gets shorter, closer to the body, or lighter. **Two bad days in one week cut that week's axial hard sets by 40%.** Intensity is held; it is the volume that comes down.

**Exit gates**

## Phase 2 — Progressive loading (8–16 weeks)

- The deadlift returns to the floor via blocks: 4" → 2" → floor, one step per two clean weeks `[proposed]`.
- Progression by double progression or APRE. Deload every 4–6 weeks: volume down 40–60%, intensity held `[proposed]`. <Source id="schoenfeld-volume" />
- Bodyweight-multiple gates below are read off an estimated 1RM from a recent working set, never a tested max `[proposed]`. The [tracker spreadsheet](/templates/) computes Epley e1RM on every logged set.

- A separate weekly axial hard-set budget. The spike guard is your trailing-30-day single-session maximum, not an acute:chronic workload ratio, which does not hold up `[contested]`.

**Exit gates**

<Gate phase={2} />

## Phase 3 — Strength build (6–12 months)

- Block periodization: accumulation → intensification → realization, one block of 4–6 weeks each `[proposed]`. <Source id="schoenfeld-volume" /> Deloads and the hard-set budget from Phase 2 carry over unchanged.
- Strongman implements enter as regressed variants first: yoke walk before yoke-and-turn, sandbag to lap before sandbag over bar, stone to a high platform before a low one `[heuristic]`. Each new implement starts at the bottom of the regression rule and earns its range, moment arm and load in that order.
- Marker toward elite: a powerlifting DOTS around 450 is roughly the top 1% of competitive open entries `[heuristic]`. Treat it as approximate and multi-year, not a gate. The [tracker spreadsheet](/templates/) computes DOTS from a total and bodyweight.

**Exit gates**

## Phase 4 — Performance (ongoing)

Meet prep, peaking and event-specific loading. The rules never retire; the ceilings rise. The red-flag screen, the daily check-in, the regression rule and the hard-set budget all stay exactly as written in Phases 0–2. No exit gate: this phase is the plan running as designed.

## Standing note

**One physiotherapy assessment is the cheapest input** that can turn guesses about *your* back into findings. The plan works without it, and says so at every gate above; nothing in it waits on a diagnosis. If you can get one, get it before Phase 1 and give the clinician this page.
