---
source: overley-2016
claim: Most elite athletes return to play after lumbar microdiscectomy, and the return rate is comparable to that reported after non-operative care.
design: Meta-analysis of studies reporting return to play in elite athletes after lumbar microdiscectomy for disc herniation.
population: Elite and professional athletes, predominantly in North American contact and power sports, treated with microdiscectomy.
effect: Pooled return-to-play rates were in the low to mid 80% range after microdiscectomy, with return typically within months of surgery; career length and performance after return were not consistently worse than in comparison groups.
certainty: low
caveats: Observational studies with selection by surgeon and athlete, heterogeneous sports and definitions of return, and limited data on non-operative comparators within the same cohorts. Read alongside sedrak-2021, which pooled both treatment arms and found no significant difference. The result argues against assuming surgery is required to return to heavy sport, not against surgery when indicated.
