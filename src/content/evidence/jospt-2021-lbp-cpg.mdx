---
source: jospt-2021-lbp-cpg
claim: For chronic low back pain, clinicians should use exercise training that targets trunk muscle strengthening and endurance (Grade A recommendation).
design: Clinical practice guideline (APTA Academy of Orthopaedic Physical Therapy), systematic review of RCTs and meta-analyses graded on strength of evidence; 2021 revision of the 2012 guideline.
population: Adults with acute, subacute or chronic low back pain, with or without leg pain, seen in physical therapy settings.
effect: Trunk strengthening and endurance exercise carries the guideline's strongest grade (A) for chronic LBP, alongside Grade A for general exercise and aerobic/multimodal programmes; specific-exercise approaches and manual therapy receive weaker grades. The guideline recommends against bed rest and passive-only care.
certainty: high
caveats: Grades reflect the consistency and quality of trials, not the size of effect; between-exercise-mode differences in trials are generally small. Recommendations are for the physical therapy setting and do not define dose or specific exercises.
