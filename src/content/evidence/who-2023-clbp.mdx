---
source: who-2023-clbp
claim: Structured exercise programmes, patient education and some psychological therapies are recommended for chronic primary low back pain in adults; several common passive and pharmacological options are recommended against.
design: WHO guideline developed with GRADE methodology by a Guideline Development Group, based on systematic reviews of RCTs; published December 2023.
population: Adults (including older adults) with chronic primary low back pain of over 3 months, managed in primary and community care settings.
effect: Conditional recommendations in favour of education and advice, structured exercise programmes, needling therapies, spinal manipulative therapy, massage and cognitive behavioural therapy. Recommendations against lumbar braces, traction, TENS, opioids, and some other drugs. Most evidence was rated low or very low certainty.
certainty: low
topic: treatment
kind: guideline
caveats: Most recommendations are conditional and the underlying evidence was graded low to very low certainty. The guideline covers chronic primary LBP only and excludes specific causes (fracture, infection, malignancy, radiculopathy with deficit). Effect sizes for exercise versus other active care are small.
