---
source: kent-2023-restore
claim: Cognitive functional therapy produced large, durable reductions in activity limitation for chronic disabling low back pain compared with usual care.
design: Three-arm, parallel-group, randomised controlled trial (phase 3), 52-week follow-up.
population: 492 adults in Australia with low back pain for over 3 months and at least moderate activity limitation.
effect: Mean difference in RMDQ activity limitation at 13 weeks was about −4.6 (CFT only) and −4.7 (CFT plus biofeedback) vs usual care; effects held at 52 weeks. Movement-sensor biofeedback added nothing.
certainty: high
caveats: Usual care was heterogeneous and unblinded participants; clinicians were trained and supervised intensively, so routine-practice effects may be smaller. Single country.
