# The science of building strength and mobility

A doctoral-style thesis on how strength and mobility are built in healthy, trained adults, from
the current evidence (searched through November 2026). It stands alone: it is not tied to any
rehabilitation plan in this repo.

**Status:** in progress. Research and verification are done for all ten literature chapters;
chapters 2 and 3 are written. The rest are being written as light first drafts, one step an
hour by a scheduled task. The plan and step queue are in [`outline.md`](outline.md).

## Method

1. Ten literature chapters, researched in parallel. Each researcher reads full texts where open
   access allows (PubMed Central, Europe PMC), favours 2020–2026 meta-analyses and trials, sweeps
   specifically for 2025–2026 work, and records every source in `/evidence/sources/`.
2. An independent verifier per chapter checks the most load-bearing claims against the full text
   and tries to refute them, correcting the evidence records.
3. A writer per chapter turns notes and verification into the chapter.
4. A synthesis chapter, an introduction and methods chapter, an evidence-record audit, and a final
   editorial pass for coherence.
