# The site writes no explanatory prose; sources speak, the site points

Readers must be able to trust that nothing here is medical education written by AI. So every sentence that
explains anatomy, pain, pathology, rehab or training is a verbatim Excerpt from a free, named Source, or a link
to one, and a build check proves each Excerpt and Homework answer appears word for word in a pinned copy of its
Source. AI may draft only Homework questions and navigation copy. This replaced ~63,000 words of AI-written
lessons, the AI-written glossary and evidence summaries, and the invented `/plan` (2 of 43 elements were
faithful to a source).

## Consequences

- Paywalled Sources, including abstract-only ones and registration walls, are cut from the site entirely.
- When Sources disagree, both are shown side by side; the site never adjudicates.
- Topics with no free source can't have a lesson, however important they are.
