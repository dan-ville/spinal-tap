# Spinal Tap

A reading companion for lumbar spine anatomy, pain science and training. The site teaches nothing in its own
words: every explanatory sentence belongs to a named Source, and the site's job is to point at it well.

## Language

**Source**:
A published textbook, guideline, trial or paper that the site cites by id.
_Avoid_: reference, citation (a citation is a pointer *to* a Source)

**Lesson**:
An ordered set of Readings followed by Homework on one topic. A Lesson has no prose of its own.
_Avoid_: article, module

**Reading**:
One assigned piece of a Source, named with a pinpoint locator (section, heading, table, or "abstract").
Only free-to-read text can be assigned. A paywalled Source is never assigned, not even its abstract.
_Avoid_: resource, link

**Excerpt**:
A Reading shown verbatim in the app, with its credit line. Only CC BY and public-domain Sources can be
excerpted; every other Reading is a link out.
_Avoid_: summary, adaptation

**Homework**:
The questions at the end of a Lesson. AI drafts them; every answer is a verbatim quote from a Reading in that
Lesson, with its locator. Homework asks what the text says, never how it applies to the reader's own body.
_Avoid_: quiz, check, exercise

**Glossary entry**:
A term with a verbatim, credited definition taken from a Source. A term with no sourced definition is left out.
_Avoid_: definition (on its own)

**Evidence card**:
The bibliographic record of one study: citation, design, link, and for CC BY papers the abstract's conclusion
quoted verbatim. It carries no interpretation.
_Avoid_: evidence summary
